Core feature

Auto-record
every event

Draw your load and dump zones on the map. The app automatically logs each load and dump as you enter and exit — no manual input mid-job.

  • Geofence-based automatic detection
  • Live load and dump counters
  • Runs in background while you drive
  • Lock screen Live Activity for at-a-glance status
Job tracking

Full job
summary

Every job captures total loads, dumps, volume moved, duration, vehicle details, driver, odometer readings, and material type — all in one place.

  • Load counts and dump volumes
  • Start and end time with duration
  • Vehicle, driver and material details
  • Export job report as PDF
Analytics

Cycle time
analytics

Understand how efficiently your fleet is running. Cycle analytics breaks down median trip times, load-to-dump pairings, and time in each zone.

  • Median cycle time per route pairing
  • Load and dump zone breakdowns
  • Total volume by zone pair
  • Time in zones analysis
Live view

Live map
tracking

Watch loads and dumps plot in real time on the map. See exactly where events occurred, with clustered markers and zone overlays.

  • Real-time event mapping
  • Load and dump event clustering
  • Zone overlay visualisation
  • Lock screen widget with live counts
History

Complete job
history

Every job stays on record. Search and filter your history, review any previous job in detail, and keep a running total across all your work.

  • Full searchable job history
  • Multi-day job support
  • Detailed event timeline per job
  • Sleek dark mode interface
