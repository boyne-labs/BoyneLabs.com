About the Show

Wired & Hammered goes deep on the technology reshaping construction, buildings and mining — without the hype. Every episode is a practitioner-to-practitioner conversation with the founders, operators and builders driving real change.

Topics span AI on site, machinery automation, digital twins, workforce tech, project management platforms, and the business of selling into one of the world's most demanding industries.

Hosted from Brisbane, Australia — reaching a global construction technology audience.
